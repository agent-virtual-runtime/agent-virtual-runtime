# Code of Conduct

We are committed to a welcoming, respectful, and harassment-free community.

Contributors must communicate constructively, respect differing viewpoints, accept responsibility for mistakes, and focus on what is best for the project and its community. Harassment, discrimination, threats, publishing private information, and other unprofessional conduct are unacceptable.

Maintainers may remove or reject contributions and participation that violate these expectations. A full Contributor Covenant policy and an enforcement contact will be adopted before the first public release.
